What we'd suggest

  1. Check whether George meant to drop the 7pm Eastern start. That hour took 13 US bookings in the last 90 days, all at weekends.
  2. Open the US afternoon (UK 19:00 - 01:00) on 2-3 evenings a week for a month and see how fast those slots fill.
  3. Set Patrick's availability. He is assigned to every consultation calendar but got only 2 calls in 90 days.
  4. Cut the reliance on George. He takes 65% of US calls, so any day he is off removes most US evening slots.
  5. Look at the 5-day booking window. Right now leads can only see 5 days ahead, and there were only 81 open slots in that whole window.
