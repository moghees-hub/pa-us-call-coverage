When US leads book, and whether we have slots then

Prepared for George · 14 Oct 2026 · Source: GHL bookings 16 Jul - 17 Oct 2026, plus live open slots on 14 Oct

The short answer

232US and Canada bookings in the last 90 days, out of 442 total (52%).
37%of US bookings land 7pm-10pm Eastern. That is the busiest window, and it is all George.
0open slots between 2pm and 7pm Eastern, on any day. No one in the team is working those hours.

US leads book in two clusters: their late morning to lunchtime, and their evening. Within a normal US 9-5, we only offer slots up to about 2pm Eastern. The whole US afternoon is closed, which for a West Coast lead is 11am to 4pm their time.

What we'd suggest

  1. Open the US afternoon (UK 19:00 - 00:00) on 2-3 evenings a week for a month and see how fast those slots fill.
  2. Set Patrick's availability. He is assigned to every consultation calendar but got only 2 calls in 90 days.
  3. Cut the reliance on George. He takes 63% of US calls, so any day he is off removes most US evening slots.
  4. Look at the 5-day booking window. Right now leads can only see 5 days ahead, and there were only 60 open slots in that whole window.
US and Canada leads are identified by the timezone on their GHL contact. The country field is "GB" for everyone, so it cannot be used. Bookings include showed, no-show, confirmed and cancelled. Deleted appointments are excluded. Times are summer time (UK BST, US EDT). UK clocks change on 25 Oct and US clocks on 1 Nov, so for that one week the UK times shift by an hour. Open slots were read from the main consultation calendars on 14 Oct. Every round-robin calendar showed the same slots.
